In Panel~(c), simulated county M2 positive bias under default EpiEstim prior ($+0.227$ \emph{cal}, $+0.227$ \emph{high-noise}) is dominated by prior pseudo-counts under small expected counts (noise-free $Y=\mu$ gives $+0.215$; noise/zero realizations add $+0.012$; moderate prior $a=0.25$ gives $+0.157$, weak $a=0.1$ gives $+0.103$, ultra-low $a=0.01$ gives $+0.024$), while M3 estimates the $\log(Y+1)$ TWFE slope rather than $\tau+\kappa$.
